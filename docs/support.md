# Support

## Email

Questions and problems: **[support@decnetgames.com](mailto:support@decnetgames.com)**

When you write, include:

- your **Unity version**,
- the **kit name** from your Kit Profile,
- the **build report text** from the Build section.

That is usually enough to reproduce the issue.

## Before you write

- The Build button stays off and says why — check the
  [FAQ]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/faq/).
- Plan errors name the field — the full list is in
  [Plan JSON Format]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/planning/plan-json/).
- Check the honest scope list — if the workflow is on
  [What v1 Is Not]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/what-v1-is-not/), it is not in
  this version; tell us which missing item matters to you.
