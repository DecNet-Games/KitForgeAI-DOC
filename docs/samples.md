# Samples

Three samples ship with the package. Run Setup once and they are imported
into `Assets/KitForge AI Samples/` — or import them individually from
Package Manager.

- [Fixture Kit]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/samples/fixture-kit/) — the included 11-piece modular snap kit on a 2 m grid.
- [Sample Outpost]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/samples/sample-outpost/) — a ready-made plan and scene: your first standing structure.
- [API Example]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/samples/api-example/) — a headless C# driver that runs the whole workflow from code.
