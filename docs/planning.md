# Planning

A **plan** is data: rooms on the grid, door and window marks on named edges,
a roof choice, and a seed. KitForge AI gives you three ways to make one —
and all three produce the same plan object.

- [The Plan Canvas]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/planning/plan-canvas/) — draw rooms on the grid, click edges to mark doors and windows.
- [Text Plan Grammar]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/planning/text-plan-grammar/) — describe the structure in one short line of text.
- [Plan JSON Format]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/planning/plan-json/) — the versioned `kitforge-plan` v1 format, field by field.
- [Rooms, Roles & Edge Marks]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/planning/rooms-roles-and-edges/) — the reference for room roles, edge sides, and marks.

![Three ways to make a plan, all in the real window]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/showcase-06.jpg)

> **Pro Tip:** Whichever way you author a plan, the window shows the plan summary in plain language before you build — footprint, floors, doors, windows, and roof. What you read is what gets built.
