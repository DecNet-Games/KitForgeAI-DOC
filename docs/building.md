# Building

Press **Build Structure** and the plan becomes a standing structure: native
instances of your kit's prefabs, placed on the kit's grid, under one
structure root — and removable with one Undo.

- [Building & Assembly Rules]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/building/assembly-rules/) — how walls, corners, openings, floors, and roofs are placed, and what happens when a piece is missing.
- [Interior Dressing]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/building/interior-dressing/) — how rooms get furnished from your kit's own Prop pieces, and how to re-dress without touching the shell.
- [The Build Report]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/building/build-report/) — the census of what was placed, what was skipped and why, and the determinism hash.
